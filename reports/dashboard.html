Guardrails around every run

  • Allowlist: only labeled sandbox services can be faulted.
  • Preflight: healthy traffic and owner data are required before injection.
  • Watchdog: errors, p95 latency, throughput, and correctness are monitored.
  • Time limit and dead-man's switch: faults are reverted even if the controller exits.
  • Kill switch: an operator can stop all active faults.

Recorded limit: 300s maximum fault duration; 3 consecutive threshold breaches to abort.
